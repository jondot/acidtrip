# TheDraw fonts

`tdfiglet-0225b8881d06773b92d9db2c7881f03ff0bdece7.zip` is a byte-for-byte
copy of the [tdfiglet](https://github.com/tat3r/tdfiglet) repository at
commit `0225b88`, the pack acidtrip's "Download more TheDraw fonts" installs.
It is kept here as a backup, so the fonts stay available if that repository
goes away. acidtrip downloads it from tdfiglet first and falls back to this
copy, and refuses either unless its SHA-256 is:

    f01deb2f165e10dcc8b39b4c7fd81a23cac020dbdc4c5df905cd2c3361870737

## Where the fonts come from

The ~1,200 `.tdf` files are TheDraw fonts drawn by artists of the 1990s BBS
and ANSI art scene. tat3r gathered them for tdfiglet, and most of them are
also in Roy/SAC's archive of TheDraw fonts at
[roysac.com](https://www.roysac.com/thedrawfonts-tdf.html).

## License

None. The fonts were released into the scene as freeware, without a license,
and neither tdfiglet nor Roy/SAC's archive attaches one. Copyright in each
font stays with its author. They are redistributed here the way the scene has
always passed them around, for use with a TheDraw-compatible editor.

They are **not** covered by acidtrip's license. If you made one of these
fonts and want it taken out, open an issue and it will be removed.
