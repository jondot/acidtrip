## Browsing

<kbd>Ctrl-F</kbd> (or palette › *Gallery: browse ANSI art…*) browses scene art like a streaming service: rows of posters, the arrows or the mouse to move, <kbd>Enter</kbd> to open.

![The Gallery home screen with rows of posters](/shots/gallery-home.png "The Gallery: rows of posters, from your own pieces to every year on 16colo.rs.")

## Home rows

- What you opened recently.
- Your collection.
- Your local folders; <kbd>+</kbd> adds one.
- Famous groups.
- Every year on 16colo.rs, newest first.

Posters are real pixel renders on terminals with graphics (iTerm2, kitty, sixel), and half-block previews elsewhere. They load in the background, what's on screen first.

A pack, a group, an artist or a folder opens as a grid. <kbd>/</kbd> searches packs, groups and artists on 16colo.rs, plus your own pieces.

## A piece, full screen

A piece opens full screen with its credits and SAUCE. Scroll it, or press <kbd>P</kbd> to play it at 14400 baud.

![A piece open full screen in the Gallery viewer](/shots/gallery-viewer.png "A piece full screen, with its credits.")

| Key | What it does |
|---|---|
| <kbd>Enter</kbd> | open it as an untitled document to remix; the credits stay in SAUCE |
| <kbd>S</kbd> | send it (or a whole pack) to the [sourcing studio](/docs/studio/) |
| <kbd>C</kbd> | save a copy to your collection, which works offline |
| <kbd>T</kbd> | take a part of it |

To take a part, drag a box over the part you want. It becomes a floating paste in your art, with its colors fitted to your document.

## Gallery and Studio tabs

The Gallery and the sourcing studio are two tabs of one window. Click a tab on its top border or press <kbd>Ctrl-T</kbd>; <kbd>S</kbd> on a piece opens the Studio tab on it.

## The GALLERY panel

The GALLERY panel in the sidebar keeps the pieces you've viewed full screen, as a strip of small pictures. They are cached, so it works offline. Click one to select it and again to view it; ‹ › scroll. *✂ take* opens it to take a part, and *gallery* / *studio* open the window on either tab.
